# Credits

Virtual Hiking Trails is built almost entirely from other people's fieldwork.
Every plant, rock and patch of ground you walk over was photographed and
scanned by somebody; every bird you hear was recorded by somebody standing in
a wood at four in the morning. This file names them.

## Ground, plants and rocks — Poly Haven (CC0)

All 3D models and ground textures are CC0 photoscans from
[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com). CC0 places no obligation on us; the

The originals are raw scan density — several are hundreds of megabytes of
vertex data — and are decimated, welded, atlas-compressed and quantized by
`tools/process-assets.mjs` before shipping. `tools/assets.json` records the
exact slug, resolution and triangle budget used for each.

## Everything else

Wind, leaf rustle, running water, rain and footsteps are **synthesised at
runtime** in `src/audio/soundscape.ts` — filtered noise shaped by the same
terrain sample the renderer uses — so nothing loops and nothing is sampled.
The convolution reverb's impulse response is generated rather than recorded.

Terrain, weather, the star field, the atmosphere model, the scatter
distribution and all shaders are original work in this repository.
